How to Play

  1. Build wealth. Grow your portfolio and finish the 24-month journey in good financial shape.
  2. Manage monthly cash flow. Salary is added and expenses are deducted each month; low cash can force investment sales.
  3. Prepare for life events. Some events change your finances automatically, while others ask you to choose.
  4. Trade thoughtfully. Buy or sell assets, select a reason, and track your decisions in the journal.
  5. Reach goals and protect your health score. Build reserves, diversify, and keep an eye on your financial health gauge.
